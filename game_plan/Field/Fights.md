The player will get a heads up from Spiro's drone, the drone will start flying, and the camera can show the player where the NPCs are hiding as they fly their own drones. The player then can move closer to try and sneak up on the NPC without getting spotted by a drone, and can win the fight by shooting/tazing the NPC until they are incapacitated. Not using the drone will leave the player walking blind as NPC drones can see the player, leading the NPCs to know where the player is and attack.

The player has a highly advanced drone that gathers information and returns as well as sensing opponents. This Spiro drone they start with gives them enough edge to survive and establish themselves, later they will learn how to build their own drones at that level/
