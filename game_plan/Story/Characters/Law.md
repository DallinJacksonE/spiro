Problems with Power
- Player joins their clan after passing the citizenship rights.
- Gains access to clan stores, inns, and has a symbol and token that gives the player access to clan homes and regions.

A large man with a 5 drone swarm, has united the regions families into a clan underneath him. While kind, he is the ultimate authority, which leads to problems within the clan as others vie for his place. The player will take the clan and Law becomes a general and advisor to the player.
