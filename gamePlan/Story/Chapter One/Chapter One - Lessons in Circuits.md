The player has been told how to build a drone and has fixed the one left by Spiro. The player sees chapter one begin, and heads back out into the field.

### First Encounter
The player, following a map by Spiro to a parts stash, meets an NPC and gets into an altercation in the road. The NPC's hidden drone appears and injures the player. The player wins the fight with Spiro's old drone, and the NPC runs after the fight after recognizing the drone. The player finds the parts and takes the NPC's drone and heads back to the workshop.

Fights are established 

### Problems with Power
The player adds a component to Spiro's drone and the drone is under powered. The player fixes the salvaged drone from the First Encounter fight, and takes the two drones out after removing the component from Spiro's drone. The player arms themselves and follows the map to a small town. They encounter two NPC battles on the road.

The player finds a town held together by the Law, a large man who keeps the peace as a sheriff. The player can join the clan an have access to the clan resources, or stake out on their own in the region outside the town. If they fight a clan member, Law comes to their aid after a certain amount of time and ends the fight.

The player struggles in the brutality of lone wolfing as others pursue them until they join the clan.

They swear fealty to the Law and have access to power and components. They are given a token and sign that let them pass through Clan settlements and into fortifications.

The player returns to the workshop and learns about voltages and power usage. They now see the power consumption of components and can replace batteries. ()

They can mount Spiro's drone with a better battery, improving the flight time and capacity. 

They are contacted by Law and given tasks to complete:
- Fight for the farm - NPC fight with three NPCs in an orchard
- Survey the deer population - scan the woods to count the deer
- Find the lost clan member on the mountain

The player notices that people are grateful for law, but they feel like they don't get much say if Law doesn't agree with them. Law confides in the player the issues with trying to balance all the needs himself, and the pressures he faces from the powerful clan families.

### Resistance
The player gets a new battery and components that have a higher voltage than the circuit he has. Player learns about resistors and gets a kit from the clan. In the workshop the player can now fix and upgrade the power to the other drones they've collected and can use those along with Spiro's drone, they are FPV. These drones last much longer than Spiro's drone, but the player sees the drone recieve a "wireless update" and Spiro's drone can now path plan and fly UAV.

After learning resistors, the player follows a map to a cave. They find clan members planning to overthrow Law. They do find new components in the cave and sneak out.

### Controllers
At the workshop the player can learn about controllers and how to wire power to controller to motor. The player gets radioed by Law to come to the village.

The player waits for Law and overhears two "travellers" reveal that they have been jealous of the clan's wealth and have spread rumors and seeded the resistance to the clan. The player learns their will be an attempt on Law's family that night. 

Law meets the player and tells the player he would like them to be a clan officer and help keep the peace. The player tells Law what they have overheard and they prepare for the attack. The player is the only available officer with Law since the other officers have gone to deal with a diversion.

The player is stationed and scans for the attackers, Law responds with a drone for each attacker the player finds in the woods. If the player's position is made and they are surrounded then the fight is lost, else the player wins. The player is able to use Spiro's path planning drone to circle areas close by while the player drives a FPV drone manually through the field.

The player is given access to the clan officer workshop.
